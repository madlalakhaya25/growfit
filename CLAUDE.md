# Growfit FA — Academy Context

## The Academy

**Growfit Sports Academy** (also "Growfit FA" in the platform) is a real, running South African grassroots football academy based in **Greater Durban**, registered as an **NPC (Non-Profit Company)**.

- **Founded:** 2023 — 3 years running
- **Age groups:** U11 · U13 · U15
- **League:** Greater Durban Football League (GDFL)
- **Weekly schedule:** Training on Wednesday and Friday; Matches on Sunday

## Platform Purpose

The platform (Growfit FA) digitises and scales what the academy does manually today. Key jobs it does:

1. **Registration** — 6 documents required per player per season (Registration Agreement, POPIA consent, photo/media consent, medical consent, code of ethics, SAFA/GDFL registration confirmation). The platform's document hub tracks all 6.
2. **Attendance** — Coaches mark P/A/L/E every session. The 75% attendance threshold triggers a welfare check-in.
3. **Player development** — 5 milestone categories (Technical, Tactical, Physical, Mental, Leadership), tracked per season.
4. **Match & training management** — Fixtures, squad selection, ratings (out of 5), post-match reports.
5. **Communication** — Announcements broadcast to squads; parents see only what's relevant to their child.
6. **Compliance** — SAFA NDP document dashboard; academy health AI report.

## Legal & Compliance Context

- **POPIA** (Protection of Personal Information Act) governs all player data (names, ID numbers, medical info, photos). Never store these in WhatsApp groups.
- **Children's Act (38 of 2005)** drives safeguarding and consent sections.
- **SAFA / LFA** registration is mandatory to compete. Every player needs a SAFA registration number.
- **CPR Part B check** (criminal record check) is legally required for every coach.
- The academy is an **NPC** — CIPC annual returns and SARS registration must stay current.

## Terminology

- **SAFA** — South African Football Association
- **LFA** — Local Football Association
- **GDFL** — Greater Durban Football League
- **MYSAFA** — SAFA's online player registration system
- **LTPD** — Long-Term Player Development (FIFA framework)
- **NDP** — SAFA National Development Programme
- **4-Corner Model** — FIFA coaching methodology (Technical, Tactical, Physical, Psychological)
- **NPC** — Non-Profit Company (the legal entity)
- **POPIA** — Protection of Personal Information Act
- **CPR Part B** — Criminal record check required for anyone working with children

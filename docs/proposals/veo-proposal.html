1Executive summary

Professional academies use match video every week to show players what they did and how to do it better. Grassroots players in Greater Durban almost never see themselves play. At about R80,000 for the first year, an AI camera system is out of reach for a volunteer-run club, so most clubs in the GDFL have no video at all.

Growfit Sports Academy is a non-profit academy running U12, U15 and U17 squads, now in its third year. We are asking for R83,500 to buy a Veo Cam 3 system and fund its first year of software. The camera films a whole match on its own from a tripod. It needs no operator, and the AI produces the broadcast-style video, highlights and match statistics within hours.

What the funding achieves

  • Player development: every Growfit match is filmed and reviewed by coaches. Short clips are used at Wednesday and Friday training, so 60-odd players learn from their own games.
  • Visibility for talent: players whose parents have given media consent get highlight reels, which can support high-school sport scholarships and trials.
  • Raising the level of the league: opposition clubs can buy the recording of their own match, giving other Durban clubs access to video for the first time.
  • Sustainability: a priced service (match footage, parent highlight packages, tournament filming and a local sponsor) is projected to cover 94% of Year-2 running costs in the base case, and more than all of them in the strong case.

How this differs from the earlier draft. This version prices the hardware and software from Veo's 2026 list prices instead of estimates. It budgets for Veo Analytics, which the parent highlight service depends on, and for insurance, data, import VAT and a contingency. The revenue streams are made POPIA-compliant: children's footage is only shared with consent, and only with the club responsible for those children. The return is presented as a path to self-sustaining operation, not as a short-term profit.

A lower-cost Option C at R29,700, using an XbotGo Falcon camera with no subscription, is set out in Section 9.

3The need

For players

Players learn faster when they see what happened. Right now, feedback after a game depends on what a coach remembers from the touchline. Video turns "keep your shape" into a clip the player can watch.

For families

High-school sports programmes and scouts increasingly ask for footage. Families in our community can't pay for private filming, so talented players stay unseen.

For the league

Most GDFL clubs are volunteer-run and have no video. A shared camera service lets the clubs we play against use their own match footage for coaching.

The barrier

The barrier is money, not interest. The hardware and first-year software for an AI camera cost about R80,000, and the subscription renews every year. One-off funding plus a service income solves both parts of the problem.

4The solution: Veo Cam 3

Veo is the most widely used AI camera in youth football. Leagues such as MLS NEXT, US Youth Soccer and US Club Soccer have adopted it as their video partner. The camera is put up on a tall tripod at the halfway line, records the whole pitch, and uploads the footage after the match. Veo's AI then produces the edited video and the data.

ComponentWhat it does for Growfit
Veo Cam 3Two 4K sensors record the full pitch in panorama. AI produces a 1080p "follow-cam" view that tracks the ball. Up to 6.5 hours of battery, enough for three back-to-back age-group games. Weighs 1.25 kg and uploads over Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. No camera operator needed.
Veo Editor (in the subscription)Shots and goals are clipped automatically. Coaches add their own clips, tags and comments, and draw on the video (arrows, shapes, spotlight). An interactive view lets them pan and zoom around the full pitch for 90 days after the match. Clips download as MP4 or share by link, and viewers don't need a Veo account.
Veo Analytics (add-on)AI-generated match statistics: possession, passes, shots, a 2D tactical view, heatmaps, shot and pass maps and a momentum graph. Per-player physical data covers distance, sprints and top speed. Player Moments reads shirt numbers to build an individual highlight reel for each player automatically. Coach Assist is an AI chat that answers questions about the team's match data.
Share with OpponentVeo's built-in feature for giving the opposing club its own copy of the recording, without our internal notes. Access can be withdrawn at any time. This is the delivery channel for the club footage service.
League Exchange (future)A shared video library for a whole league. It is a natural next step to propose to the GDFL once the service is established.
Veo Live (not in budget)Live-streaming with score and sponsor overlays. Deliberately left out of Year 1 and only to be considered if a sponsor funds it.

Why Veo, and not a cheaper camera

We compared Veo with other systems. Pixellot is built for venues and leagues and costs more. Trace is leased rather than owned. XbotGo Falcon costs about R21,000 locally with no subscription, but it is a pan-and-zoom camera with no automatic match statistics and no per-player reels. It is set out as a lower-cost Option C in Section 9. Veo's automatic Player Moments reels and match statistics drive two of the four income streams. Its built-in opponent sharing is what makes the club service practical for volunteers to run.

10Child protection, POPIA & consent

Every player we film is a child. Under POPIA (sections 34–35), children's personal information, including identifiable video, may only be processed with the consent of a parent or guardian, or under a narrow legal exception. The programme is built around that rule.

RuleHow it is applied
Consent before footage is usedGrowfit's registration pack already includes POPIA and photo/media consent. Clips and reels are only created and shared for players with consent on file, and the Growfit FA platform enforces this.
Opposition playersOpposition footage goes only to the opposition club, for its own players. The club signs a short Footage Sharing Agreement confirming it holds media consent and will not publish the footage. We do not sell individual reels of opposition players.
League & venue noticeThe GDFL is informed before launch. Pre-match notice to the opponent and a sign at the venue that filming is taking place. A match is not filmed if the league or the opposition objects.
Private by defaultRecordings are set to Restricted in Veo, not Public. Nothing is published on social media without consent, and children are never identified by full name with their video.
Retention & deletionRecordings are kept for up to 12 months (Veo plan storage), then deleted unless needed for safeguarding. Parents can ask for their child's clips to be removed, and a removal also withdraws copies shared with other clubs.
SafeguardingCamera handled only by vetted adults (CPR Part B checked). Recording is only of matches and training, never in changing rooms. The incoming Safeguarding Officer will be the named lead for video.
Information OfficerThe Academy Director serves as Information Officer and will be registered with the Information Regulator before launch. Cross-border storage happens on Veo's cloud under its data-processing terms, and this is disclosed in the consent form.

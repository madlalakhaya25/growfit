Part A · Sponsorship proposal

1Who we are

Growfit Sports Academy is a registered Non-Profit Company founded in 2023 in Greater Durban. We run U12, U15 and U17 teams in the Greater Durban Football League, training on Wednesdays and Fridays and playing on Sundays. The academy is run by three volunteer coaches. We develop players technically, tactically, physically, mentally and as leaders, and we keep children in sport through the years when many drop out.

What we stand for

  • Every child plays. Development comes before results, and game time is shared fairly.
  • Every child is safe. Child Protection & Safeguarding policy under the Children's Act, police-cleared coaches, and POPIA-compliant handling of player information and images.
  • School comes first. Our Attendance Policy and welfare check-ins mean a child who drops away gets a call, not a penalty.
  • Football opens doors. We help players toward school sports programmes, scholarships and higher-level teams.

5Our commitments to partners

  • Child-appropriate partners only. We do not accept sponsorship from alcohol or tobacco/vaping brands. Licensed betting partners are welcome. Hollywoodbets already supports our kit. On children's kit and material, any partner's branding is limited to name and logo, with no promotions aimed at under-18s, in line with gambling-advertising rules.
  • Children's images are protected. Sponsors may share academy photos and video only where we have confirmed parent/guardian consent, and never with a child's full name. Children don't appear in a sponsor's own advertising without separate written consent.
  • Money is accounted for. Funds go into the academy's bank account, are spent on what was agreed, and are reported back. Unused funds are carried forward with the partner's agreement.
  • Agreed in writing. Every partnership is confirmed in a short agreement (Template S5).

Part B · Templates for the Growfit team

How to use these templates

  • Research first. Personalise every message: name the person, mention something specific about the business (local branch, past community work, a parent who works there).
  • Start warm. Begin with parents' employers, local businesses near our pitches, suppliers we already buy from and businesses with B-BBEE targets.
  • Ask for something specific. Name one package and amount. Offer a smaller option (Squad Partner or Sponsor a Player) in the same message.
  • Follow up once after 7–10 days (S4). Then move on politely, and keep a tracker of who was contacted, when and what they said.
  • Thank and report. Send a thank-you within 48 hours (S7) and report on time. Happy sponsors renew.

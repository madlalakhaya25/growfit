2Rules we follow

TopicPractice
ReceiptsEvery gift gets an acknowledgement (D7) within 7 days, numbered in sequence and recorded in the donation register.
Tax certificatesA section 18A tax certificate is only issued if SARS has approved Growfit for 18A and the gift is used for an approved activity. Sport on its own generally doesn't qualify, so never promise a tax deduction until this is confirmed with our accountant.
B-BBEECorporate gifts for sports development may count as socio-economic development. Use the SED beneficiary letter (Sponsorship pack, Template S6).
Donor data (POPIA)Donor details are kept only in the donation register, used only to receipt, thank and update donors, and never shared. Donors can ask to be removed at any time.
Children's imagesAppeals use photos only of players with media consent, and never with full names. No child is singled out as "needy".
Raffles & lotteriesRaffles and lucky draws fall under the Lotteries Act. Check the society-lottery rules with the National Lotteries Commission before running one.
Restricted giftsIf a donor names a purpose (e.g. "for the U12s' kit"), the money is used for that purpose and recorded separately.
Who can acceptMoney only goes into the academy bank account. No cash collections by individuals, except at events with two people counting and signing.

Template D1 · Personal appeal (family, friends, community, diaspora)

Personal is best. Send by email or WhatsApp, or print and hand over. Change the story paragraph to something real from this season.

Dear [Name],

I'm writing to ask for your help with something close to my heart. In 2023 we started Growfit Sports Academy, a non-profit football academy in Greater Durban. Today about [60] young people play for our U12, U15 and U17 teams in the Greater Durban Football League.

[Story: one real, consented, non-identifying moment, e.g. "Last month one of our U12s, who joined unable to play a full match, captained the team for the first time. His mother told us he now does his homework before training so he never misses a session."]

For many families, the cost of a season (registration, kit and transport) is out of reach. R1,500 keeps one child playing for a whole season. Any amount helps:

  • R150 buys a size-4 match ball
  • R500 covers a squad's transport to an away match
  • R1,500 sponsors one player for a season
  • R250 a month as a Friend of Growfit keeps training equipment stocked all year

You can give by EFT to [bank details], reference [your surname], or online at [crowdfunding link]. Every gift is acknowledged, and I'll send you an update on what it made possible.

Thank you for thinking of our players.

Warm regards,

[Name], Growfit Sports Academy NPC · [phone]

Template D9 · Grant application cover letter

For the National Lotteries Commission (sport & recreation), the KZN Department of Sport and Recreation, eThekwini Municipality grants-in-aid and corporate foundations. Apply only when a call is open, follow each funder's own form and guidelines, and never pay anyone for application forms. Note that the NLC does not fund the same organisation again within 12 months of a grant.

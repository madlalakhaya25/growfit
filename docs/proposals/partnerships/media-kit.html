Why it works for local brands

  • The same audience, every week. League football runs about 9 months a year, so families see your brand at every match, training session and school run, not just once.
  • Goodwill attaches to your name. Parents notice who supports their children, and many are deciding where their household shops.
  • The business network. Our parents include business owners and employees of Durban companies, and our partners meet each other (Section 7).
  • It's measurable. We count attendance, track social reach and give every partner a trackable family offer (Section 8).

8What every partner gets beyond the logo

Growfit family offers

Give the families of our ~60 players, plus our partner school community, a discount or offer with a unique code or QR (e.g. "GROWFIT10"). We share it on match days and in parent groups, and you can count every customer it brings in.

Partner breakfasts

Twice a year, all partners meet the academy and each other: business owners, corporate CSI teams and suppliers in one room. Many sponsorships pay off through these introductions.

Featured directory listing

Free featured listing in the Growfit Business Directory, printed and shared digitally with all families and partner clubs.

Cross-promotion

Partners appear together at the Youth Cup and in posts. For example, the hydration partner's water point sits next to the kit partner's banner.

Staff engagement

Volunteering days at camps and the Youth Cup, a coaching clinic for staff's children, and a team in the corporate 5-a-side cup.

B-BBEE & reporting

A socio-economic development beneficiary letter, proof of contribution and a quarterly report with photos and numbers.

10Method & assumptions

We'd rather give a sponsor honest estimates than impressive ones. These figures are planning estimates for a full season, with about 60 players in 3–4 squads. They will be replaced by measured figures (Section 9) after the first season with a partner.

ItemAssumption
League matchesA minimum of 12 league matches per division (U12, U15, U17), so ~36 a season. ~80 people see the kit per match: two squads (~30), parents of both teams (~30), officials, and spectators at shared venues.
Training3 squads, 2 sessions a week, ~40 weeks, ~15 parents and passers-by per session at public fields.
Youth Cup24 teams, ~360 players, ~700 parents and spectators, plus officials and vendors: ~1,100 per tournament, twice a year.

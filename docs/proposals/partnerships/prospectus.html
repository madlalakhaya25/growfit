1The opportunity in one page

Growfit Sports Academy is a registered non-profit football academy in Greater Durban, founded in 2023 and run in partnership with North Crest Primary School. We develop young players from U12 to U17 in the Greater Durban Football League, under eight adopted policies including child safeguarding. Families trust us with their children every week.

Next season we field three divisions: U12, U15 and U17. Each plays at least 12 league matches and aims to enter about three tournaments, so around nine tournament appearances a year in front of new crowds. Most of our families come from disadvantaged backgrounds, and tournament entry fees are the biggest thing holding us back. Partner support pays those fees, and puts your brand on every one of those stages.

We are also building something bigger: a year-round calendar of league football, holiday camps, the Growfit Youth Cup, a national tour to Gauteng, and a professional social media and content programme that puts our partners in front of thousands of local households every month.

Reach

Players, families, a 700-plus-learner school community, 108 clubs in our LFA, tournament crowds and online followers. Up to ~255,000 impressions a year (target).

Trust

Parents remember who backed their children. Your brand sits next to something families care about deeply, and they see it every week.

Return

Trackable family offers, business introductions, content for your channels, staff engagement and B-BBEE socio-economic development, all reported quarterly.

Proof that it works

  • Hollywoodbets already backs our players with match kit [confirm permission to name].
  • Similar Durban grassroots events, like the Siyakhula U15 Cup (AmaZulu Community Trust and Hollywoodbets), and our own LFA's partnership with Mr Price Sport, show that local brands see value in grassroots football.
  • Our operations are run to a written standard: SAFA-registered players, police-cleared coaches, consent and POPIA compliance, and separate accounts for every project.

How to win a partner

  1. Warm first. A parent, teacher or friend who knows the decision-maker beats any cold email. Ask: "Who do you know at…?"
  2. Lead with them, not us. Open with their customers, their community and their goals. Then the children.
  3. One clear ask, one smaller option. "Kit Partner at R25,000, or Squad Partner at R8,000."
  4. Show, don't tell. Bring the kit, a photo book, the media kit and a 30-second video of the kids.
  5. Follow up three times (day 3, 7 and 14), each time with something new, then close politely.
  6. Make yes easy. Leave the one-page commitment form (O7). Offer monthly payments.

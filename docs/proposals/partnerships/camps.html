Growfit Holiday Football Camps

Four-day school-holiday football camps for boys and girls aged 6–14, open to every child in the community. The camps are coached by Growfit's police-cleared coaches, run four times a year, and bring in a steady income for the academy. They also set up the Programmes & Camps Coordinator role.

1The idea

School holidays are long, and many working parents in Greater Durban struggle to find safe, active things for their children to do. Growfit already has the coaches, equipment, policies and experience to run good football sessions. A holiday camp puts these to work during the weeks with no league football. Children have a great week, families get affordable supervised care, and the academy earns income to support its players all year.

A day at camp

TimeActivity
08:00–08:30Sign-in by a parent or authorised adult. Name tags, water bottles filled.
08:30–09:00Warm-up games: movement, coordination and fun
09:00–10:00Skill of the day, one theme per day: Day 1 dribbling, Day 2 passing, Day 3 shooting, Day 4 defending & goalkeeping
10:00–10:30Snack and shade break, plus a short talk (fair play, healthy eating, school and sport)
10:30–11:30Small-sided games that use the skill of the day
11:30–12:30Camp "World Cup": mixed teams, each named after a country, playing for points all week
12:30–13:00Cool-down, awards of the day, sign-out to an authorised adult only

Day 4 ends with a mini-tournament, certificates and a family showcase at 12:15. Every child gets a camp T-shirt and certificate, and awards go to Most Improved, Best Team-mate and Skill Star.

Who it's for

  • Any child aged 6–14, beginner or experienced, boys and girls. Children are grouped by age and ability.
  • Growfit players get a 15% discount and U17 players aged 15–16 help as junior assistants.
  • 5 bursary places per camp for children from families who can't pay, offered through local schools and community partners. Sponsors can fund extra places at R850 each.

Break-even and decision point

  • The camp breaks even at about 32 paying children.
  • Two weeks before the camp, if fewer than 28 children are booked, the camp is shortened to 3 days or cancelled with full refunds.
  • A sponsor covering T-shirts (R4,300) raises the surplus to about R10,900.

Planning estimates. Confirm the venue, T-shirt and insurance quotes before the first camp.

3The Programmes & Camps Coordinator

This is one of Growfit's three open seats. The camps give the role a clear purpose and a stipend, which makes it easier to recruit for.

AreaResponsibilities
PlanningSet camp dates around the KZN school calendar. Book the venue, first aider and insurance. Order T-shirts and snacks.
Bookings & moneyRun bookings and payments by EFT or SnapScan, keep the register, issue invoices and refunds, and manage bursary places with schools.

RequirementsOrganised, good with parents, police/CPR Part B clearance, available in the holidays. Stipend: R1,500 per camp.

4Safety & safeguarding

Camp children are not all Growfit members, so every camp has its own registration, consent and collection rules.

AreaHow it is handled
Registration & consentEvery child has a completed registration form before Day 1: parent consent, medical information, allergies, emergency contacts, authorised collectors and photo consent.
CollectionChildren are signed out only to adults named on the form, and ID is checked if the coach doesn't know the person. There is a clear process if no one collects a child.
StaffAll coaches and helpers hold police/CPR Part B clearance. Junior assistants (15–16) are always supervised and never alone with younger children. Ratio: one coach to at most 12 children.
MedicalFirst aider on site all day. Allergy list held by each group coach. Heat plan with shade, water breaks and lighter sessions in hot weather. Concussion rule: "if in doubt, sit them out".
SafeguardingA named safeguarding lead at every camp. Toilets are visited in pairs or with a same-gender adult nearby. Concerns are reported under Growfit's Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy.
Photos (POPIA)Only children with photo consent appear in photos, and never with full names. Camp registration data is kept securely and deleted 12 months after the camp unless the family joins the academy.

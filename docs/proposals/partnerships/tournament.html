1The idea

During the school holidays, grassroots players in Durban have few chances to play competitive football. The Growfit Youth Cup fills that gap. It is a one-day tournament for U12, U15 and U17 teams, run to the same standards Growfit applies every week: qualified referees, first aid on site, safeguarding, and every child getting meaningful game time.

Format

ElementDetail
Group stageTwo groups of four per age group. Every team plays three group matches.
KnockoutsGroup winners and runners-up play semi-finals, the final and a 3rd-place play-off. Teams finishing 3rd and 4th play placement matches for 5th–8th, so nobody goes home after three games.
Match lengthU12: 2 × 10 min (9-a-side). U15 and U17: 2 × 10 min (11-a-side). One pitch per division, three full-size pitches in total. Final lengths are confirmed with the sanctioning body.
SquadsUp to 14 players per team, with rolling substitutions. SAFA/LFA-registered players only, with a team list and age verification at check-in.
AwardsTrophies and medals, plus real prizes: equipment vouchers, a new match kit for each winning team, boots and gloves for individual winners, and a camp bursary for the most promising player in each division (Section 4). Every player gets a participation certificate.
ExtrasA coaches' breakfast and short coaching talk. Match filming if the academy camera is in place. A family zone with food vendors.

Why Growfit can deliver it

Growfit is a registered NPC running three age groups in the Greater Durban Football League. It has eight adopted policies, including Child Protection & Safeguarding and Injury & Medical Emergency. Every player is registered with SAFA and every coach is CPR Part B checked. Our weekly matchday routine (registration checks, attendance, first aid, communication with parents) is the same routine a tournament needs, only bigger.

2What participating clubs get

Quality football

At least four competitive matches per team in one day, against opponents of similar age from across Durban. That is more game time than a month of league weekends.

Affordable

R800 per team covers referees, medals, certificates, first aid and drinking water for players. That works out to about R60 per player for a full day of football.

Safe and well run

Qualified referees, a first-aid station, a named safety officer, a safeguarding lead and a lost-child point. Parents know what to expect.

Visibility for players

Individual awards, certificates and match footage (where available) help players with trials and school sports programmes. Scouts and school coaches are invited.

Coach development

A coaches' breakfast with a short talk (e.g. "Game-time for every player") and a chance to connect with other grassroots coaches.

Recognition

Each club is named in the programme, on social media and in the results sheet sent to all participants.

7Safety, safeguarding & compliance

AreaHow it is handled
SanctioningThe tournament is registered with the Greater Durban LFA / SAFA eThekwini Region, and their rules and referee appointments are followed.
Event safety (SASREA)The event's risk category is confirmed with the municipality and SAPS under the Safety at Sports and Recreational Events Act. In all cases: a named safety officer, a written safety plan (crowd, parking, emergency medical, evacuation) and a briefing for all marshals.
MedicalParamedic or qualified first-aid standby all day. Each team submits medical and emergency details. Concussion protocol: "if in doubt, sit them out". The nearest hospital route is in the safety plan.
SafeguardingA named safeguarding lead. Team officials are accredited and wear tags, and only accredited adults are allowed in player areas. Lost-child point, no changing-room access for spectators, and a clear process for reporting concerns.
Photos & filming (POPIA)Entry form confirms each club holds media consent for its players. Filming signage at the venue. Players without consent are not featured. Footage is shared only with the player's own club.
Weather & heatShaded areas, water points and drinks breaks in hot weather. A lightning and severe-weather stop rule. A rain-date plan is agreed with the venue.
Discipline & fair playCode of conduct for players, officials and parents. A tournament committee rules on disputes. Zero tolerance for abuse of referees.
MoneyEntry fees by EFT only, with reference = team name. Vendors pay in advance. Income and spending are reported to sponsors within 30 days.

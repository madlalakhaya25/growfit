Rules for every idea

  • Money goes straight into the academy account (EFT / SnapScan). Any cash is counted by two people, signed for, and deposited within 2 working days.
  • Children's photos only with media consent, and never with full names.
  • Donor and participant details (POPIA) are used only for the event and receipts, and never shared or sold.
  • No raffles or lucky draws without first checking the Lotteries Act rules with the National Lotteries Commission. Auctions of donated items and ticketed events are fine.
  • No alcohol sales by Growfit. Events are alcohol-free, or the venue's own licensed bar is responsible.
  • Each event gets a short report afterwards: income, costs, net, lessons, thank-yous.

1Parents' business directory

Many Growfit parents and supporters run businesses: plumbers, hair salons, caterers, taxi owners, accountants, spaza shops. A Growfit Business Directory lists them for the whole Growfit community, with a printed copy handed out at matches and a digital version shared as a link or PDF. Listing fees support the academy, and families support each other's businesses.

How it works

  1. Announce at the parent meeting and on WhatsApp. Collect listing forms (P1) over 3 weeks.
  2. A volunteer designs the directory: one line per standard listing, a box with logo per featured listing.
  3. Print 200 copies. Share the PDF link with all parents, players' families and partner clubs.
  4. Update every 6 months, with listings running 12 months.

2Corporate 5-a-side challenge (or family fun run)

A Saturday-afternoon 5-a-side tournament for company teams at an indoor arena or on a school field. Businesses pay to enter a team of staff, compete for the "Growfit Corporate Cup", and support youth football at the same time. It's a team-building day for them and a fundraiser for us. Growfit coaches referee or manage, and older players help run the event.

How it works

  1. Book the venue 10 weeks before and secure an event sponsor.
  2. Invite 25–30 companies (P2): parents' employers, suppliers, sponsors, local businesses.
  3. 12 teams of up to 8 staff. Mixed teams encouraged. 4 groups of 3, then semi-finals and a final (15 matches).
  4. Food and drinks for players included. Trophies for the winners and the "Best Dressed Team".

3"Buy a brick" equipment wall

A visual campaign: a printed wall of 90 bricks (on a banner at home matches, and online as an image updated weekly). Each brick funds a real piece of equipment. When someone buys a brick, it is coloured in with their name, or "Anonymous" if they prefer. People love watching the wall fill up, and the target is easy to understand.

How it works

  1. Print the wall banner (R800) and make a shareable image.
  2. Launch at a home match with the first bricks bought by coaches and parents. A full row early creates momentum.

5Kit swap

Children outgrow boots, shin pads, tracksuits and training kit every season. A Growfit Swap Shop at the end of the season and again at pre-season lets families pass on what no longer fits and pick up what does. It saves families money and keeps good kit in use. Income is small. The real value is savings for families and a stronger community.

How it works

  1. Announce 3 weeks ahead. Families bring clean items in good condition.
  2. Every item brought earns a token, and each token can be swapped for any item.
  3. Families who have nothing to swap can take items for a R20 donation per item, or for free on request, no questions asked.
  4. Items left over go to the boot bank (idea 6) or a partner club or school.

Rules

  • Items must be clean, washed and safe (no broken studs or torn shin-pad straps).
  • No used socks or underwear. New ones only.
  • Sort by size on labelled tables: kids' sizes and adult S–M.
  • Two volunteers run the table and handle any cash.

6Old-boots drive & boot bank

Good boots are expensive, and some children train in takkies or borrowed pairs. A 6-week Old Boots Drive collects used boots from the wider community (gyms, schools, offices, churches, sports shops), and they go into a Growfit Boot Bank. Players borrow a pair that fits for the season and return it when they outgrow it.

How it works

  1. Place labelled collection boxes at 4–6 partner sites (P6 request). Collect weekly.
  2. Clean, check and sort by size. Repair or discard unsafe pairs.
  3. Record each pair in a simple boot-bank list (size, condition, who has it).
  4. Lend for free to players who need them. Coaches refer quietly, and no one is singled out.
  5. Share surplus pairs with partner clubs and schools.

Target & value

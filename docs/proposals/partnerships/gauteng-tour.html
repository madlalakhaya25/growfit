1The opportunity

Growfit Sports Academy is a registered non-profit company founded in 2023 in Greater Durban. In [month] 2027 our U15 and U17 squads have the chance to compete at [tournament name] in [city], Gauteng, against [number] teams from [across South Africa / the region].

For most of our players, this would be their first time competing outside KwaZulu-Natal, and for some their first trip away from home. A tournament like this matters in youth football:

  • Higher competition against strong club and academy sides from other provinces.
  • Visibility in front of scouts, school sports programmes and higher-level clubs. This matters most at U17, the age when players step toward senior and professional football.
  • Life experience: travelling as a team, discipline, independence and representing their community.

Families in our community cannot cover the full cost of a trip like this. Without sponsors and donors, most of these players simply won't go.

Who we are

Growfit runs competitive teams in the Greater Durban Football League. We are governed by eight adopted policies, including Child Protection & Safeguarding (Children's Act 38 of 2005), Injury & Medical Emergency, and Codes of Conduct for players, coaches and parents. Every player is SAFA-registered and every coach is police/CPR Part B cleared. Our focus is development: every player plays, and school comes first.

Travel option: flying. Return flights Durban–Johannesburg cost roughly R2,800 per person (group fare, booked early), or R117,600 for 42 travellers instead of R54,600 by bus. That brings the total to about R362,500. Flying cuts the journey from about 8 hours to about 1 hour each way. The bus budget works out to about R7,000 per traveller. The new kit and team wear (R95,600) stays with the academy and players after the tour: match kits are used for the next two seasons, and travel wear for all future away fixtures.

Go / no-go and what happens to the money

  • Decision point: 8 weeks before departure. The tour goes ahead when at least 85% of the budget is in the bank or confirmed in writing, and travel and accommodation are booked.
  • If we fall short: first reduce the cost (fewer travel-wear items, a cheaper lodge, one kit colour), then take one squad only. Donors and sponsors are told and asked how they'd like their gift used.
  • If the tour doesn't happen: sponsors and donors can choose a refund or let their gift go to kit and equipment for the academy. Family contributions are refunded in full.
  • Any surplus goes to the academy's player-support fund and is reported to all funders.
  • All money goes into the academy's bank account under the reference "TOUR", is tracked separately, and is reported in a post-tour financial report.

5Keeping players safe on tour

Taking 36 young people on an overnight trip to another province is a big responsibility. These rules apply from departure to return and are explained to parents and players before anyone signs up.

AreaRule
ConsentSigned parent/guardian consent and indemnity, medical form, emergency contacts, copy of the player's ID or birth certificate, and a media-consent choice for every player. No form, no travel.
Supervision6 police-cleared adults for 36 players (1 : 6), with a named tour leader, a safeguarding lead and a first aider. If any player is female, a female chaperone travels.
RoomsPlayers share rooms by age group and gender. Adults never share a room with a player. Chaperones' rooms are on the same floor. Lights-out and room checks every night.
TravelScheduled, licensed bus operator (or airline). Daytime departures only, with all 42 travellers booked together and seated as a group, chaperones spread through the group, and seatbelts on. A headcount at boarding, every stop and arrival. Only licensed shuttle operators are used in Gauteng.
ConductTour code of conduct signed by players and parents. Nobody leaves the group without an adult. Zero tolerance for alcohol, smoking/vaping or bullying. A clear process if a player has to be sent home.
MedicalMedical forms carried by the first aider. Nearest hospital and clinic mapped for each venue. Travel accident insurance for all travellers. Parents called straight away for anything more than minor first aid.
CommunicationDaily WhatsApp broadcast to parents: arrival, results, wellbeing. One emergency number answered 24/7 by the tour leader.
Photos (POPIA)Only players with media consent appear in sponsor and social content, and never with full names. Player details are never shared with sponsors.

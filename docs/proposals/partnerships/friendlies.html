1The invitation

Growfit Sports Academy is a registered NPC founded in 2023, running U12, U15 and U17 teams in the Greater Durban Football League. We are inviting a small group of Durban's strongest grassroots clubs and academies to a Challenge Series: planned friendly fixtures between our clubs across all three age groups, played home and away during the season, in breaks between league rounds and in pre-season.

The idea is simple. Players improve fastest against strong, well-organised opposition. League fixtures don't always give that, and arranging good friendlies one at a time takes a lot of WhatsApp messages. A standing agreement makes it easy for both clubs.

How it works

ElementDetail
Fixture dayOne morning or afternoon, three matches back-to-back: U12, then U15, then U17. Each club travels once and all its squads play.
Home and awayHosting alternates. The host provides the pitch, referees and first aid. The visitor only arranges its own transport.
Frequency2–3 fixture days per partner per year, set at the start of the season around both clubs' league calendars.
Match rulesLeague age rules and pitch sizes. Rolling substitutions agreed in advance, so every player gets meaningful minutes. Results are recorded but the focus is development.
After the matchA 15-minute coaches' debrief between the two technical staffs. Optional joint cool-down and handshake line for players and parents.

2What your club gets

Quality, reliable fixtures

Planned games against a committed, well-organised opponent in all three age groups. They are on the calendar months ahead, so you aren't hunting for a friendly the week before.

No cost, less admin

The host covers the pitch, referees and first aid, and hosting alternates, so costs even out. One agreement replaces many separate arrangements.

Game time for the whole squad

Agreed rolling substitutions let you give minutes to development players and try new positions and systems without league points at stake.

Match footage

Where Growfit's match camera is available, your club receives the full recording of your team's matches free of charge for coaching use. It is shared only with your club, under a simple consent agreement.

Coach-to-coach exchange

A debrief after every fixture day, and an optional joint coaching session each year (e.g. on session design or game-time management), so both technical staffs learn.

Priority at the Growfit Youth Cup

Partner clubs get guaranteed places and a reduced entry fee at our twice-yearly holiday tournament, plus first offer of any joint showcase or scouting day we arrange.

What it gives Durban football

When strong clubs play each other regularly, the level rises across the city. Players get used to tougher games, coaches share ideas, and parents see grassroots clubs working together. The series is open to clubs from any part of Greater Durban, and we'd welcome partners who want to grow it into a small invitational league over time.

3Shared standards

StandardWhat both clubs commit to
Registration & ageOnly registered players within the age group. Team lists exchanged 48 hours before.
SafetyHost provides first aid on site and a referee for each match. Medical and emergency details are carried by each club for its own players.
SafeguardingAll coaches have police/CPR Part B clearance. No unaccompanied adults in player areas. Concerns reported through each club's safeguarding lead.
ConductRespect for referees, players and parents of both clubs. Both clubs' codes of conduct apply.
Photos & video (POPIA)Each club tells the other about any player who must not be filmed or photographed. Footage of a club's players is shared only with that club, and nothing is posted publicly without the other club's agreement.
ReliabilityCancellations at least 72 hours before (except weather or emergencies), with a replacement date offered.

Template F5 · Partnership agreement

A one-page agreement for the season. It is not a legal contract. It records what both clubs have agreed.

GROWFIT CHALLENGE SERIES: FRIENDLY FIXTURE AGREEMENT [season]

Between Growfit Sports Academy NPC ([reg. no.]) and [Club name] ([affiliation / reg. no.]).

1. Fixtures. The clubs will play [2/3] fixture days in the season, each with U12, U15 and U17 matches. Proposed dates: [ ]. Hosting alternates, with the first fixture hosted by [club].

2. Costs. The host club provides the pitch, a referee for each match and first aid on site. Each club pays its own travel. No fees are payable between the clubs.

3. Players. Only registered players within the age group take part. Team lists are exchanged 48 hours before. Rolling substitutions are used unless agreed otherwise, and every available player receives meaningful playing time.

4. Safeguarding & safety. All coaches hold police/CPR Part B clearance. Each club carries medical and emergency details for its own players and follows its own safeguarding policy. Concerns are reported to both clubs' safeguarding leads.

5. Photos & video. Each club confirms it holds parent/guardian media consent for players who may be filmed, and tells the other club about any player who must not be filmed. Match footage of a club's players is shared only with that club, for coaching use, and is not published without that club's written agreement.

6. Conduct & cancellations. Both clubs' codes of conduct apply. Cancellations are given at least 72 hours before (except weather or emergencies), with a replacement date offered.

7. Review. The technical directors review the series at the end of the season. Either club may withdraw with 30 days' notice.

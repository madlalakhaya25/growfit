Who we are

A registered non-profit football academy founded in 2023, playing in the Greater Durban Football League under SAFA eThekwini. We have SAFA-registered players and police-cleared coaches, and work to eight adopted policies, including child safeguarding. Hollywoodbets already backs our players with match kit [confirm permission to name].

Where your brand is seen

On the pitch

Kit, tracksuits, banners and medals at league matches, training, tournaments, camps and the Gauteng Tour. That's about 17,700 in-person sightings a season, plus about 72,000 everyday sightings of tracksuits and bags.

At North Crest

Recognition in a 700-plus-learner school community: newsletters, sports days, prize-givings. About 15,000 impressions a year. (Family-appropriate brands only, subject to school approval.)

Online

Partner funding pays for a part-time content manager: weekly match content, highlight clips and a monthly Partner Spotlight. The year-1 target is about 150,000 impressions from 3–5 posts a week.

Why your support matters

Most of our families come from disadvantaged backgrounds. Tournament entry fees, transport and kit are what hold our players back. Partner support pays for them, and every tournament we enter becomes another stage for your brand.

One partner per category: only one bank, one supermarket, one water brand, and so on. Monthly payment is available on annual packages.
